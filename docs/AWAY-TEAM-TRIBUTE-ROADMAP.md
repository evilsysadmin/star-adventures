# Roadmap: Away Team-inspired planetary missions

Status: **research and plan, 2026-11-03**. The requested tribute targets the feel and breadth of *Star Trek: Away Team* with a tactical flow inspired by XCOM: **free squad movement in the tactical view until enemies are encountered, then turn-based combat**. The original's real-time combat cannot be literally 1:1 under that decision. First-person planetary exploration remains available; the tactical view also supports free movement before combat. This requested direction supersedes the earlier combat-only tactical-view restriction, but does not waive the camera and input correctness gates. This is a roadmap, not a claim that these features already work.

## Reference mechanics and turn-based interpretation

| Original, as described in the manual | Proposed Star Adventures implementation | Remaining decision or verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Real time with a pause that accepts orders (p. 7, 10) | Free tactical squad movement while no encounter is active; detection starts a player/enemy turn sequence, with clear pending actions and previews. | Specify detection and movement interruption, then define whether combat orders resolve one by one or together. This is a deliberate adaptation. |
| Single, box, additive and numbered-group selection; 1–6 officers (pp. 7–9) | Select one or a squad, accessible roster and command groups. Start with existing three; support 1–6 only after state and layout migration. | Limits and required specialists per authored mission; group commands must not grant free movement. |
| Walk, run, crawl trade speed for noise (p. 10) | Stances change movement budget and a visible noise event; persist stance per officer and preview path cost. | Set fair, tested turn-based values; do not invent original numeric thresholds. |
| Selected guard's vision cone; sound circles and guard reactions (pp. 10–11) | Tile/geometry LOS, facing and audible events; inspection overlays and a guard state machine. Show what the player is entitled to know. | Measure occlusion and enemy behavior; distinguish visible prediction from hidden state. |
| Equipment and abilities depend on selected officers; consoles can require a specialist (pp. 8–10, 12, 24–28) | Per-officer inventory, role-gated interactions and clear failure reasons, with consumable and action costs. | Define interactions and alternatives in mission data, avoiding unwinnable rosters. |
| Briefing, team selection, main/optional evolving objectives, minimap, health and reports (pp. 9–13) | Author objectives and mission state, selection aboard ship, tactical HUD, event-derived debrief and campaign consequences. | Verify individual mission rules before claiming equivalence. |
| Target feedback communicates whether a weapon can fire or is out of reach (p. 9) | Before committing show target, range, LOS/blocker, action cost and reason for invalid shot. | Decide deterministic damage/cover and nonlethal behavior; test against actual rules. |

## Delivery sequence

Each step is a small, independently reviewable PR. Carry forward the existing board's zero-mismatch picking and accessible keyboard/touch requirements in `TACTICAL-CAMERA-GATE.md`; do not claim visual completion without actual desktop/mobile captures. Existing save versions, first-person position, crew and rollback remain supported. Before implementation, compare against current `origin/main` and open PRs and avoid duplicating work.

1. **Reference and baseline.** Observe an authorized local play session across briefing, deployment, individual/group orders, the three stances, one guard encounter, a role interaction and extraction. Record conditions, inputs, screenshots/timestamps and uncertainty outside the public repo where necessary; classify observed, documented and inferred. Capture current Star Adventures journeys and tests as baseline. **Accept:** a traceable mechanic-by-mechanic comparison and prioritized unknowns; no copyrighted screenshots/assets committed.
2. **One complete authored mission contract.** Define mission ID/version, deployment and extraction points, roster constraints, terrain/objects, primary/optional objectives, failure and debrief events. The original scenario must offer science or negotiation as a genuine route; preserve the dual mandate. **Accept:** reachable outcomes, invalid content rejected, completion and failure distinct, replayable fixture and save migration plan.
3. **Free tactical movement and encounter transition.** Define distinct free-roam, detection/transition and combat phases, officer selection, squad paths, collision and cancellation. Specify who detects whom, simultaneous contacts, stealth and encounter boundaries; stop movement at the detection point, carry exact positions/facing/health forward, and define initial turn order. No invisible relocation or free action on transition. Reuse existing tactical modules where verified behavior fits. **Accept:** deterministic replays for detection mid-path, multiple officers and enemies, no-contact movement, cancel/reissue and reload at the transition; selecting a squad cannot bypass movement or trigger checks.
4. **Combat turns and return to free movement.** Define turn ownership, action/movement budgets, target selection and enemy/environment phases in the pure rules domain. Define victory, retreat, hidden or reinforcements, and safe exit without premature combat termination; preserve positions, remaining objectives and consequences on exit. **Accept:** repeatable phase/order and replay tests, invalid moves leave state unchanged, no duplicate transition or reward, F5 round-trip in each phase and existing save regression tests.
5. **Terrain, traversal and stances.** Add walk/run/crawl, route cost, blocked cells and noise emission with stable grid coordinates. **Accept:** test each stance and terrain, route interruption and occupied cells; previews match resulting events and keyboard/touch commands.
6. **Perception and stealth.** Model guard facing, LOS/occlusion, noise propagation, awareness/investigation/alert and patrols in both free movement and combat. Define whether detection or awareness actually starts a battle. Keep player knowledge separate from hidden enemy state. **Accept:** fixed-seed replay tests for hearing, blocked sight, edge cases and alert recovery; overlays match rules without exposing hidden units.
7. **Roles, equipment and interactions.** Introduce roster selection, role capabilities, equipment uses, medical support, devices and mission objects with explicit costs. **Accept:** the same objective responds differently to at least two valid team compositions; inaccessible actions explain why; a nonviolent completion remains possible.
8. **Combat and feedback.** Add firing, target validity, range, line of sight, cover, health and incapacitation as explicit turn actions, plus a nonlethal path. **Accept:** deterministic encounters and clear invalid-action feedback; no mission success from an unrelated hostile defeat, and no soft lock on a downed officer.
9. **Ship-to-surface-to-ship vertical slice.** Brief aboard the ship, select crew, deploy, investigate/resolve optional objectives, extract and derive the public/classified reports from the same actual events. **Accept:** one complete episode with at least three genuinely distinct outcomes, persisted crew/injuries and correct debrief after refresh; failure, abort and recovery work without duplicating rewards.
10. **Presentation and parity review.** Compare authorized reference observations with the slice: framing, unit readability, overlays, command feedback, audio and pacing. Produce original art/audio and accessible alternatives. **Accept:** reviewed desktop and 360/390/430 px captures, real pointer/touch/keyboard interaction, reduced-motion behavior and measured performance; a parity matrix marks every item verified, adapted, deferred or unsupported.
11. **Expand only after the slice.** Increase roster capacity, mission variety and specialist interactions incrementally with migration and content validation. No procedural campaign or multiplayer is required to prove the tribute.

## Guardrails and next questions

- The public repository must not contain original game binaries, extracted assets, screenshots, dialogue, scripts, logos or music. Use original generic content and keep engine and authored data separate; see `CONTENT-AND-IP.md` and `AGENTS.md`.
- Tactical view now permits free movement before combat. Specify how players enter it from first-person exploration and return, preserving the position and squad state in both views; update the product and camera contracts in a focused implementation PR before shipping.
- Define squad size and action economy for the first slice, contact/detection and combat exit rules, save version, perception information boundaries, and success/failure behavior before implementation.
- Reference observations require running the game and separate runtime evidence. Until then this is a documented design roadmap, not a verified 1:1 reproduction.
